Extended+ Storage Naming System (ESNS)
Overview
The Extended+ Storage Naming System (ESNS) is a proposed naming system for digital information and storage units.
It includes standard information units and proposed additional units, including fractional, decimal, binary, and extended units.
Full Specification
The complete specification is available in ESNS-Specification-v1.0.odt in this repository.
Project Status
ESNS is a published proposal open to technical review, discussion, and implementation.
