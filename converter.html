Convert between information and storage units defined in the ESNS catalog.

Result

Calculations use exact integer ratios. Decimal results are rounded to at most 12 places after the decimal point.

About ESNS

This converter is a reference implementation for the Extended+ Storage Naming System. It includes standard information units and proposed ESNS units.

For authoritative definitions, consult the published ESNS Specification v1.0 in the project repository.
